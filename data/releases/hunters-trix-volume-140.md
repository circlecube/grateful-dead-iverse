## Notes

Hunter’s Trix is a series of fan-made matrix mixes (soundboard and audience recordings blended by Hunter Seamons). It is not an official Grateful Dead release; it is listed here as a noteworthy recording of this show.

Recording notes:
- Matrix by Hunter Seamons, 3/17/22
- SBD transfer by Charlie Miller, 5/17/17 (Cassette Master)
- AUD recording by Ted Mattes (Schoeps CMC4/MK4 > Nakamichi DMP-100)
- AUD transfer by Charlie Miller and Scott Clugston, 2/12/18
- Brent photo by Robbi Cohn
- Cover art by LEAF-INTHEWIND
- Front: https://tinyurl.com/3n2bs744
- Back: https://tinyurl.com/4bkrtb3w

Lineup:

- MICKEY HART – Drums

- PHIL LESH – Electric Bass, Vocals
- BRENT MYDLAND – Keyboards, Vocals
- BOB WEIR – Rhythm Guitar, Vocals
