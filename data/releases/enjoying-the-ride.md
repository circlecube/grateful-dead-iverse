## Notes

A limited edition 60 CD, plus one cassette, box set that includes 17 complete shows, 3 compilation taken from runs of shows, one partial show on cassette and four CDs of live bonus tracks. This set was released to mark the 60th annivrsary of the Grateful Dead. The music was also released digitally.
