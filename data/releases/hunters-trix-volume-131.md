## Notes

Hunter’s Trix is a series of fan-made matrix mixes (soundboard and audience recordings blended by Hunter Seamons). It is not an official Grateful Dead release; it is listed here as a noteworthy recording of this show.

Recording notes:
- Matrix by Hunter Seamons, 7/4/21
- SBD transfer by Charlie Miller, 7/10/10 (Dat)
- AUD recording by Ray Ackerman (AKG 460B/CK63 ULS)
- AUD transfer by Ray Ackerman, Joe Noel and Charlie Miller, 2/23/18
- Matrix review by John Bartlang
- Cover art by LEAF-INTHEWIND and Rick Griffin
- Front 1: https://tinyurl.com/2zjya6h6
- Front 2: https://tinyurl.com/3uuxdehm
- Back: https://tinyurl.com/2mekamft
