## Notes

A three CD release of all the music from the Grateful Dead show at the Fox Theatre in St. Louis, MO on December 10, 1971. This show was also released as a limited edition 5 LP set in November 2021. It was also released as part of the Listen to the River: St. Louis '71 '72 '73 CD box set.
