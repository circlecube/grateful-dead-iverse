## Notes

Hunter’s Trix is a series of fan-made matrix mixes (soundboard and audience recordings blended by Hunter Seamons). It is not an official Grateful Dead release; it is listed here as a noteworthy recording of this show.

Recording notes:
- Matrix by Hunter Seamons, 2/14/22
- SBD transfer by Dick Latvala, Rob Eaton and Charlie Miller, 11/29/20 (CM > DAT > DAT)
- AUD recording by Jim Wise (Sennheiser 421 > Sony TC-D5M)
- AUD transfer by Charlie Miller, 12/7/09
- Jerry photo by Bobbie Bestic; October sunset photo by Hunter Seamons
- Front 1: https://tinyurl.com/mr3fu3ku
- Front 2: https://tinyurl.com/2y2ra2mt
- Back: https://tinyurl.com/2xz2tdsw
