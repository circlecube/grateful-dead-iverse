## Notes

A bonus CD distributed to subscribers of the 2023 releases in the Dave's Picks series. This bonus disc was shipped with Dave's Picks Volume 46. It comprises music from two Grateful Dead shows: 9/19/72 (Roosevelt Stadium, Jersey City, NJ) and 9/3/72 (Folsom Field, Boulder, CO).
