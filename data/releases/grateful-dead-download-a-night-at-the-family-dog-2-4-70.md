## Notes

A one-off addition to the series of shows made available by downloading from the official Grateful Dead site. The release comprises the full 2/4/70 show from the Family Dog at The Great Highway that was partly broadcast on PBS. There are three bonus tracks: one from 10/5/70 and two from 12/31/70 shows. The downloads are available in both 128 & 256 MP3 and high quality lossless FLAC formats.
