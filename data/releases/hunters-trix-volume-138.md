## Notes

Hunter’s Trix is a series of fan-made matrix mixes (soundboard and audience recordings blended by Hunter Seamons). It is not an official Grateful Dead release; it is listed here as a noteworthy recording of this show.

Recording notes:
- Matrix by Hunter Seamons, 12/26/21
- SBD transfer by Charlie Miller, 6/23/20 (Master Reel > DAT)
- AUD recording by Mason B. Taylor (Nakamichi 300 > Nakamichi 550)
- AUD first generation “safety tape” transferred on a Nak. CR-7A, 5/1/14
- Joani Walker’s recording via Charlie Miller patches the AUD, 12/20/07
- Thank you to Rob Bertrando for help with pitch correction.
- Jerry photo by Jay Blakesberg, Oakland Auditorium Arena
- Front: https://tinyurl.com/2p9ypstv
- Back: https://tinyurl.com/2p88drym
