## Notes

Hunter’s Trix is a series of fan-made matrix mixes (soundboard and audience recordings blended by Hunter Seamons). It is not an official Grateful Dead release; it is listed here as a noteworthy recording of this show.

Recording notes:
- Matrix by Hunter Seamons, 12/31/22
- SBD transfer by Charlie Miller, 12/15/08 (Cassette Master > Dat)
- AUD recording by Bob Wagner (Nakamichi 700’s > Sony TC-158)
- AUD transfer by Charlie Miller and Scott Clugston, 12/13/19
- Pittsburgh photos by Dave DiCello; Jerry photo by Don Zottarelli, Stanley Theatre
- Thank you to Gary Frankel for suggesting this matrix!
- Front 1: https://tinyurl.com/2pabkvh5
- Front 2: https://tinyurl.com/ydxsd7ar
- Back: https://tinyurl.com/bdehy4s4
