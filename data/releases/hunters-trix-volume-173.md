## Notes

Hunter’s Trix is a series of fan-made matrix mixes (soundboard and audience recordings blended by Hunter Seamons). It is not an official Grateful Dead release; it is listed here as a noteworthy recording of this show.

Recording notes:
- Matrix by Hunter Seamons, 11/1/24
- SBD transfer by Charlie Miller, 11/15/11 (Cassette Master > Reel > Dat)
- AUD recording by Bob Wagner (Nakamichi CM700 mics > Sony TC-D5M)
- AUD transfer by Charlie Miller and Scott Clugston, 3/14/20
- Concert photos by Mark Currie, Ove Thompson, and unknown
