## Notes

Hunter’s Trix is a series of fan-made matrix mixes (soundboard and audience recordings blended by Hunter Seamons). It is not an official Grateful Dead release; it is listed here as a noteworthy recording of this show.

Recording notes:
- Matrix by Hunter Seamons, 12/19/22
- SBD transfer by Charlie Miller, 3/13/07 (Cassette Master)
- AUD recording by Ted Mattes (Schoeps CMC4/MK2S > Panasonic SV-250)
- AUD transfer by Charlie Miller and Scott Clugston, 6/21/18
- Thank you to Dan Raznick for suggesting this summer of ’89 matrix!
- Front 1: https://tinyurl.com/y9yae684
- Front 2: https://tinyurl.com/mucvrrdm
- Back: https://tinyurl.com/5d2kcynu
