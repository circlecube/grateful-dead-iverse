## Notes

An 80 CD / USB drive release of 30 previously unreleased Grateful Dead shows, one show from each of the thirty years between 1966 and 1995. This is a limited edition release, 6,500 CD sets & 1,000 USB drives, available only from the dead.net online store. In addition to the 30 shows the CD set includes a gold colored 7" vinyl with a 1965 Caution and a 1995 Box Of Rain (7/9/95) and a 288 page book that includes an extensive essay by Nicholas Meriwether.
