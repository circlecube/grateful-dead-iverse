## Notes

Hunter’s Trix is a series of fan-made matrix mixes (soundboard and audience recordings blended by Hunter Seamons). It is not an official Grateful Dead release; it is listed here as a noteworthy recording of this show.

Recording notes:
- Matrix by Hunter Seamons, 4/1/19
- SBD recording by Dan Healy (Ultra Matrix)
- SBD transfer by Charlie Miller, 1/30/19 (Cassette Master)
- AUD recording by Ray Ackerman (AKG 460B/CK63 ULS > Panasonic SV250)
- AUD transfer by Ray Ackerman, Joe Noel and Charlie Miller, 2/21/18
- Photo by Richard Cummings
- Thank you to David Beckwith for inspiring this matrix project and providing feedback.

Lineup:
- JERRY GARCIA – Lead Guitar, Vocals
- MICKEY HART – Drums
- BRUCE HORNSBY – Keyboards, Accordion, Vocals
