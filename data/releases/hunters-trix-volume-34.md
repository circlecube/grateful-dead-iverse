## Notes

Hunter’s Trix is a series of fan-made matrix mixes (soundboard and audience recordings blended by Hunter Seamons). It is not an official Grateful Dead release; it is listed here as a noteworthy recording of this show.

Recording notes:
- Matrix by Hunter Seamons, 11/11/08
- SBD transfer by Charlie Miller, 7/28/08 (MC > Cassette > PCM)
- AUD recording by Mark Severson (2 Sony ECM-270s on pole 35' from stage > Sony TC-158SD)
- AUD transfer by David Minches, 3/9/08
- Cover art by Nathan Smith and Omegaman

Lineup:
- JERRY GARCIA - Lead Guitar, Vocals
- MICKEY HART - Drums
- BILL KREUTZMANN - Drums
- PHIL LESH - Electric Bass, Vocals
- BRENT MYDLAND - Keyboards, Vocals
- BOB WEIR - Rhythm Guitar, Vocals
