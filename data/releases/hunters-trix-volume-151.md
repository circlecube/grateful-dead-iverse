## Notes

Hunter’s Trix is a series of fan-made matrix mixes (soundboard and audience recordings blended by Hunter Seamons). It is not an official Grateful Dead release; it is listed here as a noteworthy recording of this show.

Recording notes:
- Matrix by Hunter Seamons, 12/7/22
- SBD transfer by Charlie Miller, 4/13/18 (Cassette Master)
- AUD recording unknown; transfer by Scot Mellor and Willy, 3/23/02
- Front 1: https://tinyurl.com/349s4np7
- Front 2: https://tinyurl.com/3dmfcn8j
- Back: https://tinyurl.com/mtw3cw6e
