## Notes

Records A 17 CD box set of five Grateful Dead shows from May and June 1973. The shows included are May 13 (Des Moines), May 20 (Santa Barbara), May 26 (San Francisco), and June 9 & 10 (Washington). The box set, which is limited to 10,000 copies, also includes a poster and a tenugui. The June 10, Washington show was also separately released at the same time as a 4 CD set.
