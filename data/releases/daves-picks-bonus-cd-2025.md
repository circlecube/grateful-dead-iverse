## Notes

A bonus CD distributed to subscribers of the 2025 releases in the Dave's Picks series. This bonus disc comprises music from the Grateful Dead show at the Buffalo Memorial Auditorium in Buffalo on March 31, 1973. The bonus CD was shipped with Dave's Picks Volume 54 which comprised the complete March 26, 1973 show in Baltimore.
