## Notes

Hunter’s Trix is a series of fan-made matrix mixes (soundboard and audience recordings blended by Hunter Seamons). It is not an official Grateful Dead release; it is listed here as a noteworthy recording of this show.

Recording notes:
- Matrix by Hunter Seamons, 12/24/22
- SBD transfer by Charlie Miller, 4/29/20 (Set 1: Dat; Set 2: Cassette Master)
- AUD transfer by Pete Ebel, 3/3/09 (Schoeps CMC3/MK4 > Sony D10 Pro)
- Thank you to Dan Raznick for suggesting this summer of ’90 matrix!
- Cover art by Martin Capek
- Front 1: https://tinyurl.com/w2jz6t3p
- Front 2: https://tinyurl.com/bdep9a57
- Back: https://tinyurl.com/4hmyjhn2
