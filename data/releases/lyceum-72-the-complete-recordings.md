## Notes

/Rhino A limited edition 24 LP box set release of all the music from the 4 shows at the Lyceum in London on the Grateful Dead tour of Europe in 1972. The shows were also rereleased digitally. These shows have previously been released on CD as part of the Europe 72 box set and as individual show releases.
