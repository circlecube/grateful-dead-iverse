## Notes

An 8 show, 19 CD box set of the complete Grateful Dead shows at Tampa (4/6/78), Pembroke Pines (4/7/78), Jacksonville (4/8/78), Atlanta (4/10/78), Atlanta (4/11/78), Durham (4/12/78), Blacksburg (4/14/78) and Huntington (4/16/78). The CD box set was limited to 10,00 copies. The eight show package was also available as a digital download. The Duke University, Durham 4/12/78 show was also released as a separate 3 CD box / digital download.
