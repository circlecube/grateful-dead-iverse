## Notes

Hunter’s Trix is a series of fan-made matrix mixes (soundboard and audience recordings blended by Hunter Seamons). It is not an official Grateful Dead release; it is listed here as a noteworthy recording of this show.

Recording notes:
- Matrix by Hunter Seamons, 8/29/21
- SBD transfer by David Gans and Charlie Miller, 5/3/11 (Cassette Master > CD)
- AUD recording by Mark Severson (Nakamichi 700’s > Sony TC-D5)
- AUD transfer by Charlie Miller and Scott Clugston, 2/4/19
- Jerry photo by Jay Blakesberg
- Front 1: https://tinyurl.com/evy2dfv2
- Front 2: https://tinyurl.com/5wnnfw2a
- Back: https://tinyurl.com/24rvx5au
