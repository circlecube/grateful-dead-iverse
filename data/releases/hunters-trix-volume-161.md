## Notes

Hunter’s Trix is a series of fan-made matrix mixes (soundboard and audience recordings blended by Hunter Seamons). It is not an official Grateful Dead release; it is listed here as a noteworthy recording of this show.

Recording notes:
- Matrix by Hunter Seamons, 7/4/23
- SBD transfer by Charlie Miller, 12/28/21 (Cassette Master)
- AUD recording by Ted Mattes (B&K 4011 mics > Dat deck)
- AUD transfer by Charlie Miller and Scott Clugston, 7/12/18
- Jerry photo by Kirsten Michel, Oakland Coliseum Arena
- Front 1: https://tinyurl.com/2cnns6dh
- Front 2: https://tinyurl.com/yc33uwu6
- Back: https://tinyurl.com/4xbvw4rx
