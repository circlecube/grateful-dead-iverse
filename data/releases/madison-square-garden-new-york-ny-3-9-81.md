## Notes

/Rhino A 3 CD release of the March 9, 1981 Grateful Dead show at Madison Square Garden in New York. This show was also released, at the same time, as part of the 6 show, 17 CD set In and Out of the Garden: Madison Square Garden '81, '82, '83. A 5 LP edition of the 3/9/81 MSG show was released in October 2023.
