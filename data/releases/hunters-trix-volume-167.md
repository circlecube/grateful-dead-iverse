## Notes

Hunter’s Trix is a series of fan-made matrix mixes (soundboard and audience recordings blended by Hunter Seamons). It is not an official Grateful Dead release; it is listed here as a noteworthy recording of this show.

Recording notes:
- Matrix by Hunter Seamons, 12/27/23
- SBD transfer by Charlie Miller and neo_levo, 11/25/01 (DAT > CDR > WAV)
- AUD recording by John Ammons (Schoeps CMC32’s > Panasonic SV255)
- AUD transfer by John Ammons and Joe Noel, 4/11/17
- Thank you to Dan Raznick for suggesting this spring of ’91 matrix!

- Front 2: http://tinyurl.com/entkpu9a
- Back: http://tinyurl.com/346sd9ym
