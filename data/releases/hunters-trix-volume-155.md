## Notes

Hunter’s Trix is a series of fan-made matrix mixes (soundboard and audience recordings blended by Hunter Seamons). It is not an official Grateful Dead release; it is listed here as a noteworthy recording of this show.

Recording notes:
- Matrix by Hunter Seamons, 12/25/22
- SBD transfer by Charlie Miller, 2/4/10 (Cassette Master > Cassette)
- AUD recording by Doug Lamarre (Sennheiser 441 mics > Sony D5M)
- AUD transfer by Charlie Miller and Scott Clugston, 11/21/21
- Hampton photo by Andrea Nusinov; Brent photo by Ed Perlstein
- Thank you to Elijah Djanco for suggesting this matrix!
- Front: https://tinyurl.com/3dnu4ez6
- Back: https://tinyurl.com/2kma2xx6

- BILL KREUTZMANN – Drums
- PHIL LESH – Electric Bass, Vocals
- BRENT MYDLAND – Keyboards, Vocals
