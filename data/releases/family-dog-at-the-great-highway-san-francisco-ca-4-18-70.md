## Notes

A limited edition double LP or single CD released to coincide with the Record Store Day Black Friday event on November 29, 2013. An acoustic set originally billed as Mickey Hart and His Heartbeats and Bobby Ace and His Cards from the Bottom of the Deck. Includes five solo Pigpen tracks. Only 7500 copies were released.
