## Notes

Hunter’s Trix is a series of fan-made matrix mixes (soundboard and audience recordings blended by Hunter Seamons). It is not an official Grateful Dead release; it is listed here as a noteworthy recording of this show.

Recording notes:
- Matrix by Hunter Seamons, 9/23/08
- SBD transfer by Charlie Miller, 7/28/04 (Set 1: Cassette Master > PCM > Dat > CD; set 2 unknown)
- AUD recording by Jim and Doug Oade (Set 2: Schoeps cmc441 > Oade M118 > Oade mod SONY PCM-F1)
- AUD transfer by Doug Oade and Darrin Sacks, 6/18/04
- AUD transfer by Chris Vinci, 3/14/00 (Set 1: Nak 300 > D5 > CM > CDR)
- Cover photo of mammatus clouds in Tulsa, Oklahoma

Lineup:
- JERRY GARCIA - Lead Guitar, Vocals
- MICKEY HART - Drums
- BILL KREUTZMANN - Drums
- PHIL LESH - Electric Bass, Vocals
- BRENT MYDLAND - Keyboards, Vocals
- BOB WEIR - Rhythm Guitar, Vocals
