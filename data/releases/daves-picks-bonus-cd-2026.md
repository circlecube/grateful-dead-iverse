## Notes

A bonus CD distributed to subscribers of the 2026 releases in the Dave's Picks series. This bonus disc comprises music from the Grateful Dead show at The Omni in Atlanta, GA on December 12, 1973. The bonus CD was shipped with Dave's Picks Volume 58 which comprised the complete December 18, 1973 show in Tampa.
