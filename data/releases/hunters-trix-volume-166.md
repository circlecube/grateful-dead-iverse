## Notes

Hunter’s Trix is a series of fan-made matrix mixes (soundboard and audience recordings blended by Hunter Seamons). It is not an official Grateful Dead release; it is listed here as a noteworthy recording of this show.

Recording notes:
- Matrix by Hunter Seamons, 12/26/23
- SBD transfer by Charlie Miller, 11/14/16 (Cassette Master > Reel > Dat)
- AUD recording by Doug Lamarre (Sennheiser 441 mics > Sony D5M)
- AUD transfer by Charlie Miller & Scott Clugston, 12/21/21
- Brent photo by Mark Currie
- Front 1: http://tinyurl.com/4drwyyaf
- Front 2: http://tinyurl.com/yrxjydcu
- Back: http://tinyurl.com/3pf3d59x
