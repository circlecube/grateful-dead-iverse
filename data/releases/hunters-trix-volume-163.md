## Notes

Hunter’s Trix is a series of fan-made matrix mixes (soundboard and audience recordings blended by Hunter Seamons). It is not an official Grateful Dead release; it is listed here as a noteworthy recording of this show.

Recording notes:
- Matrix by Hunter Seamons, 8/27/23
- SBD transfer by Jim Blackwood, Jason Chastain and Steve Gravel, 7/22/23 (Pre-FOTM cut: CM > DAT > AIF)
- SBD transfer by Saint Stephen and Bill Tetzeli, 5/17/06 (Post-FOTM cut: CM > ? > DAT > CD)
- AUD recording by Mason B. Taylor, 5/18/14 (Nakamichi 300 > Nakamichi 550)
- AUD patches from Joani Walker’s recording, transfer by Charlie Miller, 12/19/07 (Sony ECM-250 > Sony TC-158)
- Jerry photo by Kirk West

- Front 2: https://tinyurl.com/ycydbrzr
- Back: https://tinyurl.com/2s43wn29
