## Notes

Hunter’s Trix is a series of fan-made matrix mixes (soundboard and audience recordings blended by Hunter Seamons). It is not an official Grateful Dead release; it is listed here as a noteworthy recording of this show.

Recording notes:
- Matrix by Hunter Seamons, 12/2/22
- SBD transfer by Charlie Miller, 4/23/18 (Set 1: CM; Set 2: CM > Reel > Dat)
- AUD recording by Frank Streeter (Sennheiser 421’s > Sony D5M)
- AUD transfer by Charlie Miller and Scott Clugston, 12/20/18
- Front 1: https://tinyurl.com/5n7uweuk
- Front 2: https://tinyurl.com/2p8ws8rm
- Back: https://tinyurl.com/347tvsr2
- Winter ’81 Box Set: https://tinyurl.com/yeypsnr2

- Front 2: https://tinyurl.com/4xsdsr3r
