## Notes

A 7 show, 20 CD box set release of Grateful Dead shows in St Louis, Missouri. The shows included in the box set are: 12/9/71 (Fox Theatre), 12/10/71 (Fox Theatre), 10/17/72 (Fox Theatre), 10/18/72 (Fox Theatre), 10/19/72 (Fox Theatre), 10/29/73 (Kiel Auditorium), and 10/30/73(Kiel Auditorium). Includes an 84 page book and "other Dead surprises".
