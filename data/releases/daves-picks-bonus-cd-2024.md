## Notes

A bonus CD distributed to subscribers of the 2024 releases in the Dave's Picks series. This bonus disc comprises music from the second set of the Grateful Dead show at the Palladium in New York City on May 4, 1977. The bonus CD was shipped with Dave's Picks Volume 50 which included all the May 3, 1977 show at the Palladium plus further tracks from the May 4, 1977 show.
