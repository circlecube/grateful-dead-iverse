## Notes

Hunter’s Trix is a series of fan-made matrix mixes (soundboard and audience recordings blended by Hunter Seamons). It is not an official Grateful Dead release; it is listed here as a noteworthy recording of this show.

Recording notes:
- Matrix by Hunter Seamons, 7/2/23
- SBD transfer by Charlie Miller, 10/15/21 (Cassette Master)
- AUD recording by Ted Mattes (Set 1: Schoeps CMC3/MK4’s > Panasonic SV-250; Set 2: AKG 414’s > Dat deck)
- AUD transfer by Charlie Miller and Scott Clugston, 7/10/18
- Jerry photo by Susana Millman, Oakland Coliseum Arena
- Front 1: https://tinyurl.com/2zdp7bkw
- Front 2: https://tinyurl.com/3cpuw88z
- Back: https://tinyurl.com/59u9tzk8
