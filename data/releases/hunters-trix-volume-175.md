## Notes

Hunter’s Trix is a series of fan-made matrix mixes (soundboard and audience recordings blended by Hunter Seamons). It is not an official Grateful Dead release; it is listed here as a noteworthy recording of this show.

Recording notes:
- Matrix by Hunter Seamons, 4/1/25
- SBD transfer by Jim Wise, Charlie Miller and Scott Clugston, 12/23/17 (Cassette Master > Dat > CD with NR > Dat)
- AUD recording by Barry Glassberg (Nakamichi 700 > Sony TCD5M, with 4:20 Drums patch via Mason B. Taylor)
- AUD transfer by John Steinthal and Matt Vernon, 11/3/03
- Sears Tower and Chicago photos by Arturo Gonzalez (thekoolaidman)

Lineup:
- JERRY GARCIA – Lead Guitar, Vocals
- MICKEY HART – Drums
- BILL KREUTZMANN – Drums
- PHIL LESH – Electric Bass, Vocals
- BRENT MYDLAND – Keyboards, Vocals
- BOB WEIR – Rhythm Guitar, Vocals
