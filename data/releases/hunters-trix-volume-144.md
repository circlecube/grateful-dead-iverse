## Notes

Hunter’s Trix is a series of fan-made matrix mixes (soundboard and audience recordings blended by Hunter Seamons). It is not an official Grateful Dead release; it is listed here as a noteworthy recording of this show.

Recording notes:
- Matrix by Hunter Seamons, 10/9/22
- SBD transfer by Charlie Miller, 8/26/06 (Dat with CM patches)
- AUD recording by Ted Mattes (Schoeps CMC3/MK4 > Panasonic SV250)
- AUD transfer by Lenny Stubbe, 9/22/13
- Concert photo by James R. Anderson
- Front 1: https://tinyurl.com/muh5xuz2
- Front 2: https://tinyurl.com/m8xa7e6
- Back: https://tinyurl.com/atrtav7d

- PHIL LESH – Electric Bass, Vocals
- BRENT MYDLAND – Keyboards, Vocals
