## Notes

Hunter’s Trix is a series of fan-made matrix mixes (soundboard and audience recordings blended by Hunter Seamons). It is not an official Grateful Dead release; it is listed here as a noteworthy recording of this show.

Recording notes:
- Matrix by Hunter Seamons, 12/10/09
- Pitch correction and EQ by Dr. Rob Bertrando
- SBD transfer by Popi N. Feeldz & Dyer O’Conner, 12/28/02 (MR > Cassette > DAT > CD)
- AUD recording by Bruce Harvie (Sony ECM 22P’s > Sony 770); transfer by Zeb & SIRMick, 1/6/06
- -
- Hunter’s Trix – The Covers (2009 Series)
- This folder contains all of the CD covers for the 28 matrixes made in 2009* (142 images), which includes the revisions for covers done over time, some revised covers for the 2008 series (24 images), all of the "pre-Trix" versions of covers for some shows (30 images), and 2 other images I put together. More to come. Additional information on the series can be found at http://www.facebook.com/pages/Hunters-Trix/130816623629200
