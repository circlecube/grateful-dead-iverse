## Notes

Hunter’s Trix is a series of fan-made matrix mixes (soundboard and audience recordings blended by Hunter Seamons). It is not an official Grateful Dead release; it is listed here as a noteworthy recording of this show.

Recording notes:
- Matrix by Hunter Seamons, 10/12/24
- SBD transfer via Bob Harrell, 3/10/02 (Cassette Master > DAT x 2 > CD, with noise reduction)
- AUD transfer via rumrunners, 5/19/09 (2 Beyer M160 > Sony TC-D5M)
- AUD unknown patch source via Andy Gustin, 1/31/00
- AUD Drums patch recordings by Rob Eaton and Mark Cohen, transferred by Charlie Miller and Scott Clugston, 6/3/24
- Concert photo by Eric Finkelman. New York skyline photos by Inga Sarda–Sorensen
- Front 1: https://tinyurl.com/2534w9xe
- Front 2: https://tinyurl.com/bd3ec8s6
- Back: https://tinyurl.com/bdzb5hdn
