## Notes

Hunter’s Trix is a series of fan-made matrix mixes (soundboard and audience recordings blended by Hunter Seamons). It is not an official Grateful Dead release; it is listed here as a noteworthy recording of this show.

Recording notes:
- Matrix by Hunter Seamons, 2/8/23
- SBD transfer by Charlie Miller, 4/15/18 (Cassette Master)
- AUD recording by Craig L. (Nakamichi 300’s > Sony TC-D5)
- AUD transfer by Andrew F., 12/3/17
- Thank you to Jack Burn for suggesting this matrix.
- Front 1: https://tinyurl.com/57pf68sj
- Front 2: https://tinyurl.com/bddmndva
- Back: https://tinyurl.com/5n6wy6dh
