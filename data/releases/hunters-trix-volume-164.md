## Notes

Hunter’s Trix is a series of fan-made matrix mixes (soundboard and audience recordings blended by Hunter Seamons). It is not an official Grateful Dead release; it is listed here as a noteworthy recording of this show.

Recording notes:
- Matrix by Hunter Seamons, 12/24/23
- SBD transfer by Charlie Miller, 2/2/12 (Cassette Master > Reel > Dat)
- AUD recording by Doug Lamarre (Sennheiser 441 mics > Sony D5M)
- AUD transfer by Charlie Miller and Scott Clugston, 12/17/21
- Concert photo by Mark Currie
- Box set: http://tinyurl.com/59prfjes
- Front 1: http://tinyurl.com/3se3wc4c
- Front 2: http://tinyurl.com/3x4evs6v
- Back: http://tinyurl.com/2zcnarmf
