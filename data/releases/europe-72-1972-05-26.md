## Notes

A 4 CD set of the complete May 26, 1972 London show remastered from the original 16 track tapes. This set was originally released as part of the Complete Europe '72 box set. This album was re-released in 2022 to coincide with the release of the Lyceum '72: The Complete Recordings vinyl box set.
