## Notes

Hunter’s Trix is a series of fan-made matrix mixes (soundboard and audience recordings blended by Hunter Seamons). It is not an official Grateful Dead release; it is listed here as a noteworthy recording of this show.

Recording notes:
- Matrix by Hunter Seamons, 10/11/24
- SBD transfer via Bob Harrell and Peter Braverman, 2/4/02 (Cassette Master > DAT x 2 > CD)
- AUD transfer via “one more shot” series, 5/27/21 (2 Beyer M160 > Sony TC-D5M)
- AUD patch source recording by Jeff Silberman, via Andy Gustin, 1/31/00 (Sennheiser 441’s > Technics PCM master)
- Concert photo by Eric Finkelman. New York skyline photos by Inga Sarda–Sorensen
- MSG ’83: https://tinyurl.com/ycxf4bvh
- Front: https://tinyurl.com/4e3yzm5f
- Back: https://tinyurl.com/6946hswc
