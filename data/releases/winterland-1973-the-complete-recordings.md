## Notes

Records A 9 CD box set of nearly "every note" played at the three Winterland shows from November 9th, 10th and 11th, 1973. The encore of the November 9 show was not recorded. Sourced from the original two track tapes of the shows. A bonus CD of music from the Cincinnati Gardens show of December 4, 1973 was included with early copies of the box set. The Winterland set was originally intended for release in 2006.
