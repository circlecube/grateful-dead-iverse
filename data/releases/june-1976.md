## Notes

A 15 CD set comprising 5 complete, previously unreleased Grateful Dead shows from June 1976. The shows are from the Boston Music Hall on the 10th and 11th June, Beacon Theatre, New York on the 14th and 15th June, and the Capitol Theatre, Passaic on the 19th June. Limited edition release of 12,000.
