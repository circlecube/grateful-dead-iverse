## Notes

The tenth in a series of shows made available by downloading from the official Grateful Dead site. The majority of the July 21, 1972 show from the Paramount Northwest Theatre, Seattle, WA. Six additional tracks from the July 22, 1972 show. The downloads are available in both 128 & 256 MP3 and high quality lossless FLAC formats.
