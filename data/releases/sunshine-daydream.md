## Notes

A remastered DVD release of the Sunshine Daydream movie plus a three CD release of all the music from the same show at the Springfield Creamery Benefit in Veneta, Oregon on August 27, 1972. Exclusive editions of the DVD/CD set, with a Blu-ray option, also include a documentary about the movie and a 40 page book. The music is available separately as a 4 LP release.
