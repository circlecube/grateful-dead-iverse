## Notes

Hunter’s Trix is a series of fan-made matrix mixes (soundboard and audience recordings blended by Hunter Seamons). It is not an official Grateful Dead release; it is listed here as a noteworthy recording of this show.

Recording notes:
- Matrix by Hunter Seamons, 12/6/22
- SBD transfer by Charlie Miller, 6/19/15 (MR > Reel > Dat to start, then Master Reel > Dat beginning with Tennesee Jed)
- AUD recordings by Richie Stankiewicz & Craig L. (Set 1: Beyer M160 > Sony TC-D5; Set 2: Nak. CM300 > Sony TC-D5)
- AUD transfers by Andrew F., 9/16/17
- Front 1: https://tinyurl.com/msyf9bku
- Front 2: https://tinyurl.com/y7nrxau4
- Back: https://tinyurl.com/2um8ucmu

Lineup:

- MICKEY HART – Drums
- BILL KREUTZMANN – Drums
- PHIL LESH – Electric Bass, Vocals
- BRENT MYDLAND – Keyboards, Vocals
- BOB WEIR – Rhythm Guitar, Vocals
