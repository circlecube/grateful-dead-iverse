## Notes

A 3 CD compilation of music from the four Fillmore West shows from February 27th to March 2nd, 1969. This is a distillation of the music released on the limited edition 10 CD complete Fillmore West recordings box set. Mixed from the 16 track master tapes. Mastered in HDCD.
