## Notes

A 4 LP set of the complete Grateful Dead show at the Capitol Theatre in Passaic, NJ on April 25, 1977. This vinyl set was released on Record Store Day in April 2016. The show was released in 2015 as part of the 30 Trips Around the Sun box set.
