## Notes

A single CD bonus disc of live music from the Des Moines, IA on June 16, 1974 and Louisville, KY on June 18, 1974. This bonus CD was distributed with early orders of Road Trips: Vol 2, Number 3 which featured music from the same two June 1974 shows.
