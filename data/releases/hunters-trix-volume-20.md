## Notes

Hunter’s Trix is a series of fan-made matrix mixes (soundboard and audience recordings blended by Hunter Seamons). It is not an official Grateful Dead release; it is listed here as a noteworthy recording of this show.

Recording notes:
- Matrix by Hunter Seamons, 9/10/08 (Set 2), 9/15/08 (Set 1)
- SBD transfer by Alan Fink and Mark Ferguson, 4/12/00 (Set 1: PCM > DAT > Sonic Solutions > CD)
- SBD transfer by Randy Griesman, 9/17/01 (Set 2: MC > DAT > CDR)
- AUD recording by Jim and Doug Oade (Schoeps CMC441s > Oade M118 > Oade mod SONY PCM-F1)
- AUD transfer by Doug Oade and Darrin Sacks, 4/6/04
- Cover art by Bill and Vaughn Zormeir

Lineup:
- JERRY GARCIA - Lead Guitar, Vocals
- MICKEY HART - Drums
- BILL KREUTZMANN - Drums
- PHIL LESH - Electric Bass, Vocals
- BRENT MYDLAND - Keyboards, Vocals
- BOB WEIR - Rhythm Guitar, Vocals
