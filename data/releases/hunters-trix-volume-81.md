## Notes

Hunter’s Trix is a series of fan-made matrix mixes (soundboard and audience recordings blended by Hunter Seamons). It is not an official Grateful Dead release; it is listed here as a noteworthy recording of this show.

Recording notes:
- Matrix by Hunter Seamons, 10/28/10
- SBD transfer by Charlie Miller, 10/16/10 (Cassette Master)
- AUD recording by Michel the Belgian Deadhead (Sennheiser > Sony TC-D5)
- AUD transfer by Michel the Belgian Deadhead, 9/21/04
- Cover photo by NASA of the central region of the Milky Way
- -
- Hunter’s Trix – The Covers (2010 Series)
- This folder contains all of the CD covers for the 17 matrixes made in 2010 (81 images), which includes the revisions for covers done over time and some revised covers for the 2008 and 2009 series (42 images). More to come.
- Additional information on the series can be found at facebook.com/GratefulDeadTrix.
