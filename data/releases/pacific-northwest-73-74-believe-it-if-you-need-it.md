## Notes

A 3 CD release of live tracks selected from the 19 CD box set Pacific Northwest '73-'74: The Complete Recordings. That set comprised six Grateful Dead shows in the Pacific Northwest in June 1973 and May 1974. The shows are: June 22, 1973, Vancouver; June 24, 1973, Portland; June 26, 1973, Seattle; May 17, 1974, Vancouver; May 19, 1974, Portland; and May 21, 1974, Seattle.
