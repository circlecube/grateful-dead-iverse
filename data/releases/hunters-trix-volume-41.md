## Notes

Hunter’s Trix is a series of fan-made matrix mixes (soundboard and audience recordings blended by Hunter Seamons). It is not an official Grateful Dead release; it is listed here as a noteworthy recording of this show.

Recording notes:
- Matrix by Hunter Seamons, 3/14/09
- SBD transfer by Charlie Miller, 11/27/07 (SBD > Cassette Master > Cassette > CD )
- AUD recording and transfer by Jim Wise, 2/8/01 (Sennheiser 421's > Sony TC-D5)
- AUD patch for Touch of Grey by Mark Cohen, transferred by David Minches, 7/24/03 (Nak 700s > Sony D5)
- Cover art by Nathan Smith and Rerti Alev

Lineup:
- JERRY GARCIA - Lead Guitar, Vocals
- MICKEY HART - Drums
- BILL KREUTZMANN - Drums
- PHIL LESH - Electric Bass, Vocals
- BRENT MYDLAND - Keyboards, Vocals
- BOB WEIR - Rhythm Guitar, Vocals
