## Notes

Hunter’s Trix is a series of fan-made matrix mixes (soundboard and audience recordings blended by Hunter Seamons). It is not an official Grateful Dead release; it is listed here as a noteworthy recording of this show.

Recording notes:
- Matrix by Hunter Seamons, 12/5/22
- SBD transfer by Charlie Miller, 2/1/11 (Cassette Master > Cassette)
- AUD recording by Richie Stankiewicz and Craig L. (Beyer M160’s > Sony TC-D5)
- AUD transfer by Andrew F., 8/28/17
- Front 1: https://tinyurl.com/2s73datv
- Front 2: https://tinyurl.com/24d7ettv
- Back: https://tinyurl.com/y46kyeb8

- MICKEY HART – Drums
- BILL KREUTZMANN – Drums
- PHIL LESH – Electric Bass, Vocals
- BRENT MYDLAND – Keyboards, Vocals
