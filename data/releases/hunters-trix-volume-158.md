## Notes

Hunter’s Trix is a series of fan-made matrix mixes (soundboard and audience recordings blended by Hunter Seamons). It is not an official Grateful Dead release; it is listed here as a noteworthy recording of this show.

Recording notes:
- Matrix by Hunter Seamons, 7/1/23
- SBD transfer by Charlie Miller, 8/6/18 (Cassette Master)
- AUD recording by Ted Mattes (Schoeps CMC3/MK4 mics > Panasonic SV-250)
- AUD transfer by Charlie Miller and Scott Clugston, 7/11/18
- Thank you to Dan Raznick for suggesting this New Year’s run.
- Front 1: https://tinyurl.com/555nr5cx
- Front 2: https://tinyurl.com/4bas2sdw
- Back: https://tinyurl.com/2p97uy7a
