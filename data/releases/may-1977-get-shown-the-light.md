## Notes

An 11 CD release of all the music from four consecutive Grateful Dead shows in May 1977: 5/5/77 New Haven, 5/7/77 Boston, 5/8/77 Cornell, and 5/9/77 Buffalo. Two versions were released. A limited edition of 15,000 numbered copies that included a book (Cornell '77: The Music, The Myth And The Magnificence Of The Grateful Dead's Concert At Barton Hall by Peter Conners) and an 'all music edition' which comprised all the music but no book. The Cornell show was also released separately on CD and vinyl. 5 LP sets of the Buffalo and Boston shows were also subsequently released.
