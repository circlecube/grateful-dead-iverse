## Notes

A 14 CD / 2 DVD release of all the music from five Grateful Dead shows at Giants Stadium in East Rutherford, NJ. The shows are July 12, 1987, July 9, 1989, July 10, 1989, June 16, 1991 and June 17, 1991. Multi-camera film of the complete June 17, 1991 performance is also included on DVD or Blu-ray.
