## Notes

Hunter’s Trix is a series of fan-made matrix mixes (soundboard and audience recordings blended by Hunter Seamons). It is not an official Grateful Dead release; it is listed here as a noteworthy recording of this show.

Recording notes:
- Matrix by Hunter Seamons, 8/7/21
- SBD transfer by Charlie Miller & Joe Samaritano, 6/30/08 (Set 1: Dat > CM > Dat; Set 2: Dat)
- AUD recording by Doug Lamarre (Sennheiser 441’s > Denon DTR-80)
- AUD transfer by Rob Berger, 11/23/17
- Matrix review by John Bartlang
- Cover art by LEAF-INTHEWIND
- Front 1: https://tinyurl.com/8fa2bhca
- Front 2: https://tinyurl.com/wpn6vjab
- Back: https://tinyurl.com/ymct2akf
