## Notes

Hunter’s Trix is a series of fan-made matrix mixes (soundboard and audience recordings blended by Hunter Seamons). It is not an official Grateful Dead release; it is listed here as a noteworthy recording of this show.

Recording notes:
- Matrix by Hunter Seamons, 10/27/21
- SBD Cassette Master to Dat transfer by Dick Latvala
- SBD transfer by Jim Wise, Charlie Miller and Scott Clugston, 3/1/21 (CM > Dat > CD w/NR > Dat)
- AUD recording by Barry Glassberg (Set 1 + Encore: Nakamichi 700 > Sony D5)
- AUD recording by Frank Streeter (Set 2: Beyer M-160 > Sony D5)
- AUD set one transfer by John Steinthal and Matt Vernon, 11/3/03
- AUD set two transfer by Charlie Miller and Scott Clugston, 11/20/18
- Brent photo by Paul Natkin, Uptown Theatre
- Sears Tower and John Hancock photos by Arturo Gonzalez (thekoolaidman)

Lineup:
- JERRY GARCIA – Lead Guitar, Vocals
- MICKEY HART – Drums
- BILL KREUTZMANN – Drums
- PHIL LESH – Electric Bass, Vocals
- BRENT MYDLAND – Keyboards, Vocals
- BOB WEIR – Rhythm Guitar, Vocals
