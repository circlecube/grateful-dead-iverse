## Notes

Hunter’s Trix is a series of fan-made matrix mixes (soundboard and audience recordings blended by Hunter Seamons). It is not an official Grateful Dead release; it is listed here as a noteworthy recording of this show.

Recording notes:
- SBD recording by Betty Cantor-Jackson
- AUD recording by Bob Wagner (Sony 54P mics > Sony TC-158)
- Matrix by Hunter Seamons, 10/31/24
- SBD transfer by Charlie Miller, 4/17/10 (Master Reel > PCM > Dat)
- AUD transfer by Charlie Miller and Scott Clugston, 11/12/19
- Concert photo by John Rottet. Cover photo by Linkovvv. Back cover photo by Gerald Berliner
- Front 1: https://tinyurl.com/2tk98enh
- Front 2: https://tinyurl.com/ynd5pwvh
- Back: https://tinyurl.com/yetma7ru
