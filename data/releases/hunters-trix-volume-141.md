## Notes

Hunter’s Trix is a series of fan-made matrix mixes (soundboard and audience recordings blended by Hunter Seamons). It is not an official Grateful Dead release; it is listed here as a noteworthy recording of this show.

Recording notes:
- Matrix by Hunter Seamons, 6/9/22
- SBD recording by Betty Cantor-Jackson; transfer by Charlie Miller, 10/20/08 (Set 1: MC > CD; Set 2: MR > Dat > CD)
- AUD recording by Steve Maizner (Sony ECM-990 > Sony TC-153); 1st gen. reel transfer by Rob Berger, 5/18/09
- Cover art by Marlon; concert photo by Grant Gouldon
