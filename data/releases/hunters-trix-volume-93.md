## Notes

Hunter’s Trix is a series of fan-made matrix mixes (soundboard and audience recordings blended by Hunter Seamons). It is not an official Grateful Dead release; it is listed here as a noteworthy recording of this show.

Recording notes:
- Matrix by Jubal H. Seamons, 12/10/11
- SBD transfer by Scott Clugston, 8/12/01 (MC > Reel > DAT > CD)
- AUD recording by Doug Oade (Schoeps CMC 441 > Sony TC D5)
- AUD transfer by Darrin Sacks, 4/12/02
- AUD patch in CR&S recorded by Greg Holtz, transferred by Jamie Waddell
- Pitch correction aid by Rob Bertrando
- Matrix creation inspired by Roberto Lewisimo, master glass blower
- Cover art by Nathan Smith

Lineup:
- JERRY GARCIA – Lead Guitar, Vocals
- MICKEY HART – Drums
- BILL KREUTZMANN – Drums
- PHIL LESH – Electric Bass, Vocals
- BRENT MYDLAND – Keyboards, Vocals
- BOB WEIR – Rhythm Guitar, Vocals
