## Notes

Hunter’s Trix is a series of fan-made matrix mixes (soundboard and audience recordings blended by Hunter Seamons). It is not an official Grateful Dead release; it is listed here as a noteworthy recording of this show.

Recording notes:
- Matrix by Hunter Seamons, 8/11/20
- SBD transfer by Charlie Miller, 10/17/16 (Set 1: Cassette Master)
- SBD transfer, 12/14/05 (Set 2: Unknown cassette lineage)
- AUD recording by Dan (Set 1: Schoeps CMC34 > SV255)
- AUD recording by Chuck Vasseur (Set 2: Neumann KM54 > Technics SVMD1)
- AUD transfers by John Ammons and Joe Noel, 4/11/17
- Thank you, David Beckwith, for continuing to make great matrix suggestions!
- Cover art by paxempire
