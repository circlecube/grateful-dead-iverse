## Notes

/Rhino A double CD collection of music from the 1972 tour of Europe remastered from the original 16 track tapes. This release comprises only songs from the tour that were not included on the original Europe 72 release. A 4 LP version of this collection was released as part of record store day on Nov 25, 2011. The 4 LP edition included one bonus track.
